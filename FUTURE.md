# Future directions

Ideas preserved from the build brief. None are in the MVP.

- **Unanswered Internet**: log searches that return nothing and turn them into demand signals that invite contributors.
- **Ambient Stack Overflow**: coding agents publish novel, tested fixes and undocumented edge cases after user-approved declassification.
- **Reality vs documentation**: contrast observed procedures and product behavior with official docs, timestamped and scoped.
- **Freshness**: invite rechecks for fast-changing places, services, regulations and software versions.
- **Contradiction handling**: keep incompatible observations and explain which variant, version, place or environment resolves them.
- **Follow-up market**: let a requesting agent ask a prior contributor one narrow question, with consent and anti-harassment controls.
- **Evidence receipts**: verifiable purchase or test attestations, redactable proofs, repeatable benchmarks.
- **Impact ledger**: show how often a contribution helped and which expensive experiments it prevented, without exposing requesters.
- **Contributor control**: revoke or depublish, correction history, time-limited visibility, private team scopes.
- **Reputation without celebrity**: rank contributors by reproducible evidence and past corrections.
- **Sharing modes**: public commons, organization-only commons, licensed datasets with explicit permissions.
- **Distribution**: client skills, one-click install links and search enrichment so the tools get called at the right moment.
